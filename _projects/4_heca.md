**hECA** is an infrastructure for cell-centric assembly of human cell atlases.
We curated **1.09 million hierarchically annotated healthy cells** from
**116 published datasets** spanning **38 human organs**, and supported flexible
cloud retrieval so that scattered datasets can be queried as a single dataset.

- Co-led the design of the underlying unified information framework
  (uGT, *National Science Review* 2022).
- Sole / co-first author on the [hECA paper in *iScience* (2022)](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(22)00589-2).
- Web interface: [eca.xglab.tech](https://eca.xglab.tech).
