## Atlas data is scattered across datasets

Single-cell data on healthy human tissue is published as many separate
datasets, each with its own format and its own cell-type names.

hECA is an infrastructure for *cell-centric* assembly: the unit of the atlas is
the cell, not the dataset.

## What we assembled

- 1.09 million healthy cells, hierarchically annotated
- from 116 published datasets
- spanning 38 human organs

Flexible cloud retrieval lets the scattered datasets be queried as a single
dataset.
