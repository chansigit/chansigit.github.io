Research Statement

My research interests lie in Machine Learning and Foundation Models for large-scale, high-dimensional scientific data. In particular, I am interested in generative pre-training on sparse count data, representation learning and geometry (optimal transport, hashing, disentangled latent spaces), and the data engineering that lets models consume millions of heterogeneous samples. Most recently, I have been building foundation models for cell-type classification and exploring LLM agents as autonomous analysts for biological data.

  • Resolving the interior of a tumor from a routine CT scan Conceived a way to describe a tumor by the layout of its interior instead of one feature vector for the whole tumor: cluster per-pixel texture features on a CT slice into regions [11]. Co-developed it into ITHscore [10], an unsupervised score of how fragmented those regions are, tested on 6 patient cohorts (n=1399) against tumor stage, tissue invasion, survival and gene-pathway activity.
  • Meniscus Atlas Led a single-cell study of the human meniscus, a fibrocartilage of the knee, that gave the first precise characterization of its cell composition, separately for the inner and outer zones, and identified the molecular changes of the degenerated meniscus [23]: new chondrocyte subtypes linked to degeneration and a loop of matrix breakdown, angiogenesis and inflammation, confirmed by imaging (12 samples, 8 patients). Designed and carried out the analysis, built the open cell browser, and wrote the first draft.
  • Statistical detection of where a microbial genome breaks its own rules Proposed D2R [5], a linear-time statistic for mining recurring subsequences from large-scale microbiome sequencing data, which helps identify programmable elements, mobile genetic elements, noncoding RNA systems, etc. It extends word-count statistics to 2nd-order self-matching (handshake) counts within a sequence. Advised by Michael S. Waterman and Fengzhu Sun. Also contributed to nuMetaSim [24], a platform for simulating microbiome sequencing data.
